The Threat Landscape

Why Retail Borrowers Fall Victim

Predatory lending syndicates exploit regulatory blindspots, spoof legitimate NBFCs, and weaponize device permissions.

🎭
NBFC Impersonation
Scam portals claim to be licensed entities like "ABC Finance Ltd." but operate on lookalike rogue domains to bypass basic trust checks.
💸
Advance Fee Demands
Borrowers are coerced into paying ₹1,500 – ₹5,000 "security deposits" or "insurance processing fees" to mule UPI accounts before loan release.
📱
Extortion Permissions
Sideloaded APKs steal device Contacts, SMS messages, Call Logs, and Photos, using them for harassment and blackmail.
⏱️
Lack of Early Warning
Victims only discover they have been defrauded after funds are sent or personal contacts are contacted by recovery syndicates.

4-Layer Parallel AI Risk Defense

LenderLens evaluates parallel evidence sources and combines them via the Risk Fusion Engine into an actionable verdict.

L1
Identity & Regulatory Cross-Check

Loan Terms & Permission Risk
Parses Key Fact Statements (KFS), evaluates APR interest disclosures, flags advance deposit demands, and scores invasive device permissions (`Contacts`, `SMS`, `Media`).

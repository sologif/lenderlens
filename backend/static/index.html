THE PROBLEM

A loan can look legitimate and still be dangerous.

Borrowers often see polished loan pages before they see the signals that matter: impersonated lenders, hidden fees, excessive permissions, suspicious domains and connected scam entities.

LenderLens puts those checks directly in the browser at the moment a borrower is evaluating a loan.

HOW IT WORKS

Four checks. One clear decision.

The extension turns fragmented fraud evidence into a borrower-friendly trust signal and a deeper analyst case when needed.

01

Identity & regulatory

Cross-check the claimed lender and domain against authoritative registration records.

Registry → identity → domain
02

Loan & permission risk

Read key loan disclosures, APR signals, urgency language, fees and invasive permission requests.

Terms → permissions → behavior
03

Temporal anomalies

Detect unusual bursts in complaints, inquiries and other time-series activity.

Sequence → velocity → anomaly
04

Fraud network

Connect domains, phone numbers and suspicious payment accounts to known risk clusters.

Graph → neighbors → syndicate

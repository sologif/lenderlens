## 60-second pitch

**Problem:** Borrowers can encounter professional-looking loan portals that hide impersonation, advance fees, invasive permissions or links to fraud networks.

**Solution:** LenderLens checks the lender and the loan page at the moment of borrowing, combines four evidence layers, and converts them into an explainable risk decision.

**Differentiator:** The browser extension protects the borrower while the analyst console gives institutions a deeper investigation workflow for uncertain or high-risk cases.
